## 📖 About  
**The Covenant** is a scripture reading app designed to provide an effortless and enriching experience. Users can explore books, chapters, and verses, receive AI-generated insights, and listen to scriptures with text-to-speech functionality.  

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/331e6200-62a9-462b-bebe-1e31053f7128" width="100%" height="auto">  

---

## ✨ Features  

✅ **Book, Chapter & Verse Selection** – Navigate scriptures with ease.  
🧠 **AI-Generated Thoughts** – Gain insights on the verse of the day.  
🔊 **Text-to-Speech Support** – Listen to scriptures anytime.  
📌 **Auto-Saving** – Resume reading from where you left off.  
📅 **Verse of the Day** – Daily scripture for reflection.  
🚀 **No Sign-In Required** – Instant access without login.  
📜 **Full Chapter Reading** – Read entire chapters seamlessly.

## 📲 Installation  

1. **Download the APK** from [Releases]([https://github.com/your-repo-link/releases](https://github.com/SebastianBenjamin/THE-COVENANT-APP/raw/refs/heads/main/The_Covenant_App.apk)).  
2. Install the APK on your Android device.  
3. Open *The Covenant* and start reading!
